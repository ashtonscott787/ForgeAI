Why bother

What you actually get for owning it.

The bill stops

A subscription for five people is about $100 a month forever. Hardware you already own costs nothing more to keep running.

Client work stays private

Contracts, prices, patient notes, student work — none of it is uploaded to be stored, logged or trained on somewhere else.

No queue, no cap

No “you've reached your limit, come back in three hours” in the middle of a job. The only limit is how fast your machine is.

Old machines earn their keep

The PC in the back room becomes a worker. Join it with a code and big jobs go to whichever computer is free.

It does whole jobs

Not just answers — it sends the reply, books the delivery, publishes the page and tells you what it did.

You can still use the big models

Paste a Claude or OpenAI key and ForgeAI routes the hard questions there, showing the cost as it goes. Best of both.

Pricing questions.

Is the free version crippled?

No. One person on one computer gets the whole app — chat, email, documents, websites, agents, training, the lot. The paid tiers are about scale: more people, more computers, and the customer-facing parts like the phone line and website chat.

What am I paying for if it runs on my computer?

The software, the updates, and someone to ask when it goes wrong. There is no server cost to pass on, which is exactly why it can be a one-off rather than a subscription.

Does the licence expire?

No. A one-off licence covers the version you bought and its updates. If you stop paying for anything, nothing switches off.

What about the cost of the AI itself?

Local models are free to run — the electricity is the only cost. If you connect Claude or ChatGPT, you pay them directly at their API rates, and ForgeAI shows you the running total so there are no surprises.

Do I need a powerful computer?

8 GB of memory runs the built-in assistant. 16 GB or more, or an NVIDIA graphics card, runs bigger models comfortably. An Apple Silicon Mac is the sweet spot.
