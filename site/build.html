From a sentence
to a live link.

Describe the page you want. ForgeAI builds it, shows it to you, and hands back a link you can send — hosted by ForgeAI or pushed straight to your own web host.

You ask

In the words you would use anyway.

No template to pick, no builder to learn. Say what the page is for and what should be on it.

It starts

The page puts itself together.

Header, hero, the words, the photo — laid out as it goes, in front of you, so you can stop it the moment it heads the wrong way.

It keeps going

Including the parts that actually do something.

A booking form, a calculator, a price list that adds up — real working pieces, not pictures of them.

It publishes

A link you can send straight away.

Hosted by ForgeAI with a private link, or pushed to Netlify, GitHub Pages or your own web space with one press.

Then changed

“Make the photo bigger and add the phone number.”

Say what is wrong and it edits the real thing. The link stays the same, so whoever you sent it to sees the new one.

In the app

Everything it has built, in one place.

Each site keeps its own link, its own files and its own history. Share it, lock it behind a key, or push it to your host.

Sites

Share links that just work

Send the link and it opens — on a phone, on a laptop, for someone with no account. Private by key if you would rather.

Or your own host

Netlify and GitHub Pages are one press away, with the token kept in the vault. Updates go out the same way.

Little apps, not just pages

A quote calculator, a stock sheet, a roster, a form that emails you. Interactive things people can actually use.

It keeps the files

The HTML is yours, on your machine. Download it, edit it by hand, or hand it to whoever does your website.

Documents too

The same trick, for paperwork.

Word documents, PDFs, slide decks and spreadsheets, built from your own material and saved into Files — ready to send.
