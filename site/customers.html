Three doors

However they reach you.

A phone number, an iMessage thread and the chat box on your website. One ForgeAI behind all three.

The phone

It picks up on the second ring.

Opening hours, prices, where to park, whether you ship — answered out loud from what you have told it, with the transcript kept.

The texts

It replies like a person would.

A booking moved on iMessage, the calendar updated behind it, and a confirmation on its way — without you unlocking your phone.

Your website

The chat box that actually knows things.

It answers from your own notes and never mentions what is behind it. When it does not know, it hands over to you instead of inventing.

All of it

Same brain. Same building.

Every answer comes from material you gave it, on hardware you own. Nothing is uploaded to be stored or trained on elsewhere.

In the app

Set the chat up in an afternoon.

Paste one line of script onto your site. Give it the knowledge it should answer from. Decide what it must never say.

Website chat

It sounds like your place

Natural typing delay, your tone, no “I am an AI assistant”. Visitors get an answer, not a disclaimer.

It will not leak its workings

A real phone number
