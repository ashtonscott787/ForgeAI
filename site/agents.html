A back office
that runs itself.

An agent is a job that keeps happening. They pick up work, hand it between each other, and bring back anything that needs a person. All on the computer under your desk.

The office

You, in the middle.

ForgeAI sits on your computer and holds the work: your mail, your files, what it has learned about the business.

The staff

Four standing jobs.

Inbox triage at seven. Invoice chasing on Friday. The website checked every morning. The week counted up for you.

The work goes out

Nobody asks you first.

Each agent takes its job and gets on with it — reading, drafting, checking, counting — while you are somewhere else.

They talk to each other

Work moves sideways too.

Triage spots an unpaid invoice and hands it to the chaser. The chaser asks the week's numbers for context. No one waits on you to pass a message.

Your bit

Only the decisions reach you.

Anything that spends money, sends something or cannot be undone stops and waits. Everything else is already done.

By breakfast

Done — and none of it left the building.

Thirty-one emails sorted, six replies drafted, two flagged. Nothing uploaded, because the model was on your machine.

In the app

Agents

On a schedule, or on a word

Approvals before anything lands

A record of every run

What it read, which model it used, how long it took and what it changed — kept on your machine.
