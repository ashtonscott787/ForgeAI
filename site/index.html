Privacy

Nothing leaves the building unless you say so.

With a local model your chats, email and files never touch the internet. No account to make, no usage sent home. The only thing ForgeAI ever uploads is what you explicitly ask a cloud model to read.

Runs offline

Pull the cable out and it keeps working. Local models need no internet at all.

Keys in a vault

Passwords, API keys and sign-in tokens are encrypted on the computer and never shown to the AI.

Every action logged

An activity log on both sides of anything crossing between people, organisations or computers.

Leaves cleanly

Uninstall removes the app. Delete data removes the data. Your choice, every time, in plain words.
